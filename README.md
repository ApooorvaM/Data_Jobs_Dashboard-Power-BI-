# 📊 Data Jobs Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI report designed to analyze the global data job market in 2024. This dashboard provides insights into job postings, salary distributions, hiring platforms, employment types, geographic demand, and degree/benefits requirements across key data roles (Data Engineers, Data Scientists, Data Analysts, etc.).

## 📌 Key Insights & Features

### 1. High-Level Metrics (KPI Cards)
* **Total Job Postings:** Analyzed over **479K+ job postings** globally.
* **Median Yearly Salary:** Total average of **$113K / year**.
* **Median Hourly Rate:** Average of **$47.62 / hour**.

### 2. Market Trends & Comparisons
* **2024 Monthly Job Posting Trends:** Tracks month-over-month fluctuations in job availability throughout 2024.
* **Hourly vs. Yearly Median Salary Scatter Plot:** Highlights role compensation levels, showing high-earning positions like **Machine Learning Engineer** and **Senior Data Engineer** vs. entry-level or analytical roles.
* **Job Counts by Role:** Visualizes total postings per title (led by Data Engineers, Data Analysts, and Data Scientists).
* **Detailed Role Breakdown Matrix:** Itemizes salary medians, hourly wages, total counts, and sparkline trends per title.

### 3. Drill-Through Analysis (Role Deep Dive)
When drilling through into a specific role (e.g., *Data Engineer*):
* **Salary Gauges:** Quick range visualization for yearly salary (e.g., $126.27K median) and hourly rate ($59.16).
* **Job Requirements & Perks (Donut Charts):**
  * **No Degree Required:** Analyzes degree dependency (~47% requiring no degree).
  * **Work From Home:** Percentage of remote opportunities (~15%).
  * **Health Insurance:** Availability of health coverage benefits (~10%).
* **Global Distribution Map:** Geolocation mapping showing posting concentrations across North America, Europe, Asia, and other regions.
* **Top Job Platforms:** Bar breakdown of primary job platforms (LinkedIn, Indeed, BeBee, ZipRecruiter, etc.).
* **Employment Types (Treemap):** Proportion of Full-time positions (~89.38%), Contractors (~8.71%), Internships, and Temporary roles.
